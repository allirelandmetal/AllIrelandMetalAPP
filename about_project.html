The Aiséirí Initiative

The **All Ireland Metal Project (AIMP)** was powerful collaborative movement, uniting over **140 Irish musicians** across all four provinces to create charity compilation albums. Founded post-COVID in 2021, AIMP's core mission is to breathe life back into the local scene while raising crucial funds for national charities. Aiséirí, meaning "Resurrection" in Irish, was the title of the three albums and in 2025 the documentary. The project has concluded. We hope you enjoy this app.

Inspired by the legendary Roadrunner United, we showcase the incredible depth of Irish metal, from black and death metal to folk and thrash, proving that community and creativity thrive even in darkness.

Our Releases (Aiséirí Volumes)

The project has successfully released multiple volumes, each featuring unique collaborations between artists from bands like **HORRENDA, ZHORA, RESTIVE NATION, WORDS THAT BURN,** and many more. Every sale directly benefits our designated charities.

Who We Support

We are dedicated to giving back. Past and present beneficiaries include **Barnardos Ireland, Women's Aid,** and **Parkinson's Ireland.** Your support funds vital community services across the island.
